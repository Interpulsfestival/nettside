The Four Seasons – Recomposed

Friday 26.02.27 · 19:00
Trondheimsolistene · Eldbjørg Hemsing · Evelyn Rasmussen Osazuwa · Guro Kleven Hagen · Philipp Geist

Eldbjørg Hemsing and the Trondheim Soloists take us through the seasons with Max Richter's Vivaldi Recomposed, woven together with music by Julia Wolfe, Jessie Montgomery, Arvo Pärt and a world premiere by Ellen Lindquist.

Evelyn Rasmussen Osazuwa spices up the evening with her own texts, and Philipp Geist's spectacular visuals turn the whole church into a living canvas for the music. The evening is divided into four short concert sets, one for each season. Some of the best places are on rugs in the middle of the floor. There are also benches and chairs around the room, and a limited number of reserved chairs for those who want a fixed seat.

  • PULS 1Spring: Ellen Lindquist: Air (world premiere) · Quenton Blache: Habari gani · Richter: Spring
  • PULS 2Summer: Richter: Summer · Osazuwa: Hun er syk · Wolfe: Fuel, 3rd movement
  • PULS 3Autumn: Richter: Autumn · Montgomery: Banner
  • PULS 4Winter: Osazuwa: Tidløs · Pärt: Fratres · Richter: Winter
  • Doors open 18:00. Duration approx. 120 minutes.
  • Tickets: unreserved NOK 450, student / under 35: NOK 270. Reserved chair NOK 590 (only 40 chairs).
