City Life and The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat

Thursday 25.02.27 · 19:00
Nordic Wind Orchestra · Interpuls Ensemble · Philipp Geist · Sofia Nesje Enger · Petter Moen · Johannes Nikolai Aas · Jakob Dingstad · Victoria Putterman · Sonia Knop

Michael Nyman's chamber opera The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat is based on neurologist Oliver Sacks' famous book. It tells of Dr P., a singer and music teacher who no longer recognises faces and objects, and who finds his way through everyday life with the help of music.

A moving and wry opera about memory, identity and the power of music, set to Nyman's driving and playful minimalist score. Duration approx. 70 minutes. Before the opera we hear Steve Reich's City Life, which captures the sounds of New York and turns them into pulsating music, performed by Interpuls Ensemble and Nordic Wind Orchestra, along with Salonen's wind quintet Memoria. The ticket covers the whole evening.

  • Steve Reich: City Life
  • Esa-Pekka Salonen: Memoria
  • OPERAMichael Nyman: The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat
  • Doors open 18:30. Duration approx. 150 minutes incl. interval.
  • Tickets: NOK 395, student / under 35: NOK 220. Unnumbered seating. The ticket covers the whole evening.
  • Conductor: Jakob Dingstad. Director: Victoria Putterman. Set and video: Sonia Knop. Singers: Sofia Nesje Enger, Petter Moen and Johannes Nikolai Aas.
