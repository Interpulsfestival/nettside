City Life and The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat

Nordic Wind Orchestra · Interpuls Ensemble · Philipp Geist · Sofia Nesje Enger · Petter Moen · Johannes Nikolai Aas · Jakob Dingstad · Victoria Putterman · Sonia Knop

Michael Nyman's chamber opera The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat is based on neurologist Oliver Sacks' famous book. It tells of Dr P., a singer and music teacher who no longer recognises faces and objects, and who finds his way through everyday life with the help of music. A moving and wry opera about memory, identity and the power of music, set to Nyman's driving and playful minimalist score. Duration approx. 70 minutes. Before the opera we hear Steve Reich's City Life, which captures the sounds of New York and turns them into pulsating music, performed by Interpuls Ensemble and Nordic Wind Orchestra, along with Salonen's wind quintet Memoria. The ticket covers the whole evening.

  • Steve Reich: City Life
  • Esa-Pekka Salonen: Memoria
  • OPERAMichael Nyman: The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat

19:00

The Four Seasons – Recomposed

Trondheimsolistene · Eldbjørg Hemsing · Evelyn Rasmussen Osazuwa · Guro Kleven Hagen · Philipp Geist

Eldbjørg Hemsing and the Trondheim Soloists take us through the seasons with Max Richter's Vivaldi Recomposed, woven together with music by Julia Wolfe, Jessie Montgomery, Arvo Pärt and a world premiere by Ellen Lindquist. Evelyn Rasmussen Osazuwa spices up the evening with her own texts, and Philipp Geist's spectacular visuals turn the whole church into a living canvas for the music. The evening is divided into four short concert sets, one for each season. Some of the best places are on rugs in the middle of the floor. There are also benches and chairs around the room, and a limited number of reserved chairs for those who want a fixed seat.

  • PULS 1Spring: Ellen Lindquist: Air (world premiere) · Quenton Blache: Habari gani · Richter: Spring
  • PULS 2Summer: Richter: Summer · Osazuwa: Hun er syk · Wolfe: Fuel, 3rd movement
  • PULS 3Autumn: Richter: Autumn · Montgomery: Banner
  • PULS 4Winter: Osazuwa: Tidløs · Pärt: Fratres · Richter: Winter

Drawing concert

Nordic Wind Orchestra · Interpuls Ensemble · Philipp Geist

A concert where the children help create what they see! Musicians from Nordic Wind Orchestra and Interpuls Ensemble play while the children draw, and Philipp Geist turns the drawings into living images on the walls of Kulturkirken Jakob. A short and colourful concert for the whole family, approx. 45 minutes. Suitable for children and adults of all ages. Crayons and paper are provided.

M is for Man, Music, Mozart and midnight opera

Veslemøy Narvesen · Torben Snekkestad · Mats Eilertsen · Henriette Eilertsen · Tora Augestad · Nordic Wind Orchestra · Andreas Hanson · Interpuls Ensemble · Philipp Geist · Sofia Nesje Enger · Petter Moen · Johannes Nikolai Aas · Jakob Dingstad · Victoria Putterman · Sonia Knop

Veslemøy Narvesen has written the new work Listener for the occasion for Interpuls Ensemble, some of the country's finest jazz musicians and herself. Nordic Wind Orchestra and their artistic director Andreas Hanson perform Louis Andriessen's iconic M is for Man, Music, Mozart with Tora Augestad as soloist, a rare chance to hear the work live. Listener invites us to do just that: to listen, to open up to each other's different perspectives and to meet the resistance that arises with curiosity. Is there room for two entirely different perspectives in one harmony? The evening ends with a midnight opera: Michael Nyman's wry chamber opera The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat, based on neurologist Oliver Sacks' famous book, with Nyman's driving, playful and surprisingly festive music, in which Schumann's music also plays an important part. The opera can be bought as a separate ticket or as part of the evening pass.

  • 21:00PULS 3Concert set with Torben Snekkestad, Mats Eilertsen, Henriette Eilertsen and Veslemøy Narvesen
  • 22:00OPERAMidnight opera: Nyman: The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat
