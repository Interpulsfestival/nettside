M is for Man, Music, Mozart and midnight opera

Saturday 27.02.27 · 19:30
Veslemøy Narvesen · Torben Snekkestad · Mats Eilertsen · Henriette Eilertsen · Tora Augestad · Nordic Wind Orchestra · Andreas Hanson · Interpuls Ensemble · Philipp Geist · Sofia Nesje Enger · Petter Moen · Johannes Nikolai Aas · Jakob Dingstad · Victoria Putterman · Sonia Knop

Veslemøy Narvesen has written a new work for the occasion for Interpuls Ensemble, some of the country's finest jazz musicians and herself. Nordic Wind Orchestra and their artistic director Andreas Hanson perform Louis Andriessen's iconic M is for Man, Music, Mozart with Tora Augestad as soloist, a rare chance to hear the work live.

The evening ends with a midnight opera: Michael Nyman's wry chamber opera The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat, based on neurologist Oliver Sacks' famous book, with Nyman's driving, playful and surprisingly festive music, in which Schumann's music also plays an important part. The opera can be bought as a separate ticket or as part of the evening pass.

  • 19:30PULS 1Veslemøy Narvesen: New commission (world premiere)
  • 20:15PULS 2Andriessen: M is for Man, Music, Mozart · Tora Augestad (voice), Nordic Wind Orchestra, conductor Andreas Hanson
  • 21:00PULS 3Concert set with Torben Snekkestad, Mats Eilertsen, Henriette Eilertsen and Veslemøy Narvesen
  • 22:00OPERAMidnight opera: Nyman: The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat
  • 19:30–23:10. Doors open 19:00, and again at approx. 21:45 for those coming only for the opera.
  • Evening pass (PULS 1–3 and midnight opera): NOK 450, student / under 35: NOK 270.
  • Evening ticket without opera (19:30–21:30): NOK 290, student / under 35: NOK 170. Midnight opera only at 22:00: NOK 290, student / under 35: NOK 170.
  • The opera: Sofia Nesje Enger, Petter Moen, Johannes Nikolai Aas and Interpuls Ensemble. Musical direction: Jakob Dingstad. Director: Victoria Putterman. Set and video: Sonia Knop.
