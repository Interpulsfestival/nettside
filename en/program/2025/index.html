20:00

Festival warm-up at Trekanten

Interpuls Ensemble · Astrid Nordstad

A taste of the festival in a relaxed setting at Trekanten in Pilestredet, with OQS Sessions. Music by Glass, Shaw, Nicolas and Sollima.

07:00

Morning Beat & yoga concert

Morning Beat · TheFunnyPack · Interpuls Ensemble · Cecilie Kristoffersen

A sober morning rave with the DJ duo TheFunnyPack, coffee and vegan breakfast. At 9 am, yoga with live music by Glass and Pärt.

  • 07:00Morning Beat with TheFunnyPack
  • 09:00Yoga with live music
19:00

FRAM & Reich

FRAM Ensemble · Henriette Eilertsen · Interpuls Ensemble · Norges musikkhøgskole · Philipp Geist

An evening of repeating rhythms, light art and meditative soundscapes, ending with Steve Reich's Music for 18 Musicians.

  • 19:00PULS 1FRAM Ensemble: Mazzoli, Monk, Tanaka and Tabakova
  • 19:45PULS 2Henriette Eilertsen, jazz flute
  • 20:30PULS 3Reich: Music for 18 Musicians · artistic direction Kjell Tore Innervik
22.02.25Saturday
13:00

Trillepuls – for the youngest

Stemmeklang · Interpuls Ensemble

A calm 35-minute concert for babies and toddlers with their grown-ups. Mats on the floor, and prams are welcome.

17:00

Feldman in the tower

Koryfe Trio

Morton Feldman's For Philip Guston, over four hours, up in the church tower. Come and go as you like.

18:00

NÅ: Glass, Allegria & Stalheim

Ensemble Allegria · Amalie Stalheim · Astrid Nordstad · NyNorsk Messingkvintett · Stemmeklang · Interpuls Ensemble · Quantaloop · Philipp Geist

Seven concert pulses of around 30 minutes, from solo to Philip Glass' Third Symphony in surround. In collaboration with Ensemble Allegria's concert series NÅ.

  • 18:00PULS 1Dessner: Aheym · Samimi Mofakham: Eternal Chasms
  • 18:45PULS 2Fisher: Komorebi · Bolstad: Vindefugl · Kilar: Orawa
  • 19:30PULS 3Shaw: in manus tuas · Futrell: DE (world premiere)
  • 20:15PULS 4Glass: Symphony No. 3 in surround
  • 21:00PULS 5Vocal Sessions with Astrid Nordstad
  • 21:45PULS 6Tabakova: Cello Concerto, with Amalie Stalheim
  • 22:30PULS 7Quantaloop live in the crypt
