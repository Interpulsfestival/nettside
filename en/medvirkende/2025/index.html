Chamber orchestra

Ensemble Allegria

Self-run ensemble founded in 2008, led by Maria Angelika Carlsen, known for playful ensemble playing and several award-winning albums.

Cello

Amalie Stalheim

Award-winning cellist from Bergen who has appeared as soloist with leading orchestras. Played Tabakova's Cello Concerto with Ensemble Allegria.

FE
Ensemble

FRAM Ensemble

An innovative ensemble that has run a concert series of music by women since 2021.

Jazz flute

Henriette Eilertsen

Norway's first jazz flautist with a bachelor's degree in jazz performance, exploring the flute's place in music today.

Trio

Koryfe Trio

A trio from Ensemble Koryfe playing new music. Played Feldman's For Philip Guston in the church tower.

NyNorsk Messingkvintett

A brass quintet that has premiered more than 50 works since 2016.

A five-voice ensemble founded by Kristin Bolstad, with a Grammy-nominated album.
