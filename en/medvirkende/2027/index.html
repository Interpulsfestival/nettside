Ensemble and organiser

Interpuls Ensemble

The musicians who organise the festival. A string quartet and percussionists at the core, and guest musicians from small to larger ensembles.

Chamber orchestra

Trondheimsolistene

One of Norway's leading chamber orchestras, founded in 1988. Artistic director: Guro Kleven Hagen.

Violin

Eldbjørg Hemsing

A household name in Norway since childhood and today one of the world's leading violinists. An exclusive Sony Classical artist with several Norwegian Grammys and Opus Klassik awards, she plays the 1707 «Rivaz, Baron Gutmann» Stradivarius, on loan from Dextra Musica. She has performed with the Philharmonia, the Detroit Symphony Orchestra and the Hong Kong Philharmonic, among others, and her violin carries the main theme of the soundtrack to Netflix's Frankenstein (2025). Artistic Director of the Oslo Chamber Music Festival.

Words and slam poetry

Evelyn Rasmussen Osazuwa

Actor and slam poet, known from the TV series Hotel Cæsar and Oljefondet, among others. In 2017 she became the first woman and the first Scandinavian to win the Slam Poetry World Cup, and she has performed at the National Theatre in Oslo, Slottsfjell and the Lillehammer Literature Festival. At Interpuls she spices up The Four Seasons with her own texts.

Violin

Guro Kleven Hagen

Artistic director of the Trondheim Soloists and first concertmaster of the Norwegian National Opera Orchestra. Plays a Bergonzi violin, and was the soloist in Philip Glass' Violin Concerto at Interpuls 2024.

Visuals

Philipp Geist

German artist and pioneer of video mapping. He has created large-scale light and video installations all over the world, and creates the visuals for the whole festival.

Wind orchestra

Nordic Wind Orchestra

Leading Nordic wind players with conductor Andreas Hanson, exploring new concert formats.

Director, opera

Victoria Putterman

Directed In the Penal Colony at Interpuls in 2022, and stages Nyman's The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat.

Photo: Erik Berg

Soprano

Sofia Nesje Enger

Soprano from Nøtterøy who graduated from the Academy of Opera at the Oslo National Academy of the Arts in 2024. She made her professional debut in 2022 as Clarice in Haydn's Il mondo della luna with the Trondheim Symphony Orchestra, and has sung with the Norwegian Radio Orchestra and the Bucharest Radio Choir and Orchestra, among others. Nominated for the Hedda Award for her role in Pelléas et Mélisande.

Photo: Erik Maxim Konda

Tenor

Petter Moen

Has sung with the Norwegian National Opera, the Royal Danish Opera, the Royal Swedish Opera, the Festival d'Aix-en-Provence and the Edinburgh International Festival, among others. His repertoire ranges from Mozart and Rossini roles such as Tamino and Count Almaviva to Wagner, Strauss and the major oratorios. He also sang in In the Penal Colony at Interpuls in 2022.

Photo: Mikolaj Chomacki

Bass

Johannes Nikolai Aas

The Norwegian bass Johannes Nikolai Aas from Skien joined the soloist ensemble of the Norwegian National Opera & Ballet in autumn 2026 on a three-year contract. He completed his master's in opera at the Oslo National Academy of the Arts in spring 2024, and holds a bachelor's in musical theatre. Between graduating and his ensemble contract he was a soloist trainee in the opera's talent programme WOYS. In recent seasons his roles have included the Sacristan in Tosca and the Monk in Don Carlo, and until January he sings Colline in the company's new La bohème. This spring he appears as Montano in Otello and Bishop Jens in Anne Pedersdotter.

Photo: Erik Berg

Set and video design, opera

Sonia Knop

Artist, filmmaker and writer working across moving image, installation and cinema. She studied at the Städelschule in Frankfurt, Cooper Union in New York and the Oslo National Academy of the Arts. Her work has been shown at the International Short Film Festival Oberhausen, the German Film Museum in Frankfurt and the Biennale Mulhouse, among others. She creates the set and video design for Nyman's chamber opera.

Photo: Christian Tviberg

Morning rave and yoga

Morning Beat & Cecilie Kristoffersen

Sober morning raves with DJs, dance and yoga. Cecilie Kristoffersen leads the yoga concert.
