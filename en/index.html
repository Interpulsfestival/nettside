About the festival

A classical concert like you've never experienced one

Interpuls is an annual festival at Kulturkirken Jakob, created by musicians who live and breathe for sharing music.

Popcorn, an open bar, Persian rugs and hammocks, and visuals filling the whole church. Sit at a café table, lie on a rug close to the musicians, do yoga to live music after the morning rave, or comes and goes between short concert sets.

More about Interpuls

The format

An evening at Interpuls

Concert pulses

Short sets of max. 30 minutes with breaks in between. Come and go, have a drink, talk, listen.

The room as stage

The whole church is used, from the tower to the crypt. Choose a chair, a café table or a rug close to the musicians.

Visuals

German artist Philipp Geist creates new projections for every piece on the church walls.

Morning rave and yoga

With Morning Beat we dance from 7 am, followed by a yoga concert with live music.

For all ages

A children's concert for the youngest, and pop-up concerts in public spaces.

New music

At least one new commission every year, alongside the classics of minimalism.

Audience

Almost 3,500 people have experienced Interpuls since 2020. Around half don't usually go to classical concerts, and two in three are under 50.
